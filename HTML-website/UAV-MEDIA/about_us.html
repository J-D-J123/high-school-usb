    FAQ


  1. What drones do you use?
    2. We use the DJI Mini 2 because the drone weighs less than 249 grams
    and it is easy to take with you. It also has a flight time up to 2 hours.
    It has a wind resistance of 38 kph making it stable in the air.

  2. What type of media can you take?
    3. We can take videos from 1080p to 4k.
    The still photo resolution is 12 MP.

  3. What happens when the drone or the controller run out of battery life?
    4. The drone has a return to home function that will automatically return
    to the home point when the battery is low. If the controller runs
    out of battery the drone will also automatically return to the home point.

  4. When does the home base point update?
    5. The home base point updates when the drone launches.

  5. What is your turn around time?
    6. We think its great when clients give us a two-week notice prior to a shoot.
    Sometime in the next week after the shoot we will send you a link to the photos
    and or video as well as send you a printed version of the photos.

  6. Are all the pictures on your site taken from your drones?
    7. Yes, all of the pictures that you see on our site are taken from our drones.
